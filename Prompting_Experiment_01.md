# Prompting Experiment 01 — Iterative Prompt Design

## Objective

Explore how changing the structure and specificity of a prompt can improve the usefulness of an AI-generated product marketing concept.

## Scenario

Create a launch concept for a fictional generative AI API designed for developers and creators who want to generate images and short-form video.

## Prompt Version 1

Create a marketing campaign for a generative AI API that helps developers and creators generate images and videos.

## Evaluation Criteria

I would evaluate the result based on:

- Clarity of the target audience
- Strength of the value proposition
- Specificity of the use cases
- Differentiation from generic AI messaging
- Quality of the call to action
- Whether the concept would be useful to developers
- Whether the concept would also appeal to creators

## Prompt Version 2

You are a product marketer launching a generative AI API for developers and creative teams.

Develop a concise launch campaign aimed at two primary audiences:

1. Developers who want reliable generative media capabilities through an API.
2. Creators and creative teams who want to turn ideas into images and short-form video faster.

The campaign should communicate:

- The customer problem
- The product capability
- Three concrete use cases
- A clear value proposition
- One memorable campaign message
- One developer-focused content idea
- One creator-focused social content idea
- A simple call to action

Avoid vague claims such as "revolutionary" or "the future of AI."

Prioritize specificity, clarity, practical use cases, and customer outcomes.

## What Changed?

Version 2 adds:

- A defined role for the AI
- Clearly defined audiences
- Explicit output requirements
- Customer problems and outcomes
- Constraints against generic marketing language
- Separate developer and creator perspectives

## Key Learning

A useful prompt is not simply a question.

It can function as a structured brief containing:

**Role → Audience → Problem → Objective → Constraints → Required output → Evaluation criteria**

This structure makes it easier to evaluate whether an AI response actually solves the intended marketing problem.

## Product Marketing Connection

The same principle applies when marketing AI products:

**Technical capability → Customer problem → Use case → Outcome → Story → Action**

The goal is not to describe what the technology can do.

The goal is to make the technology understandable, relevant, and actionable for the person who needs it.
